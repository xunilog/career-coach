# Company Research

Generate a detailed research report on any company with one click.

## Generating Research

![Company research report](/screenshots/company-research.png)

1. Open a job from your results.
2. Click **Research Company** in the job detail view.
3. The AI generates a 5-section report.

## Report Sections

| Section        | Content                                                         |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Overview**   | Industry, company size, funding stage, headquarters             |
| **Culture**    | Company values, work environment, employee sentiment            |
| **News**       | Recent announcements, product launches, layoffs, funding rounds |
| **Key People** | Leadership team, hiring manager info (when available)           |
| **Market**     | Product & market position, competitors, growth trajectory       |

## When to Use Research

Research is a **prerequisite** for resume and cover letter generation — the Writer agent uses it to tailor your documents to the company's context and values.

It's also useful on its own before an interview or when deciding whether to apply — having the company's recent news, culture, and market position at a glance helps you make informed decisions.

## Important Notes

- Research is **manual only** — it never runs automatically. This avoids unnecessary API costs.
- **Re-generating** research overwrites the previous report for that job. Do this if the previous report feels stale or incomplete.
- Research is stored locally. You can view it any time by reopening the job.
- If research **fails** (API error, timeout), you'll see an error message. Click Research Company again to retry.
