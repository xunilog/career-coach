# Work Experience

![Experience entry with STAR and RACI details](/screenshots/experience-entry.png)

Enter your past roles with rich detail. The **Experience Coach** agent helps you articulate each position using the STAR and RACI frameworks — turning bullet points into compelling stories that power resume generation.

## How It Works

1. Open the **Profile** section in the app.
2. For each past role, enter the company, title, dates, and key achievements.
3. Start a conversation with the Experience Coach from within a role entry.
4. The coach asks structured questions to draw out depth — context, actions, results, and scope of responsibility.

## Frameworks

### STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

The Experience Coach uses STAR to help you structure achievements:

| Element    | What to capture                                       |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Situation  | The context and challenge you faced                   |
| Task       | What needed to be done                                |
| Action     | What you specifically did (skills, decisions, leadership) |
| Result     | Quantifiable outcomes and impact                      |

### RACI (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed)

For each role, the coach also helps you define your scope:

| Role        | Meaning                                                         |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Responsible | Work you personally delivered                                   |
| Accountable | Decisions you owned and were answerable for                     |
| Consulted   | Areas where your input was sought before decisions were made    |
| Informed    | Topics you needed to stay aware of but didn't directly drive    |

This gives the generation pipeline a clear picture of your actual scope and influence — not just your title.

## Why It Matters

Well-structured experiences are the raw material for resume generation. The Writer agent draws from your STAR stories and RACI scope to produce tailored, specific bullets — not generic job descriptions. Vague experiences produce vague resumes.
